# Hours

## Front desk
- Monday to Friday: 9 AM to 5 PM Pacific
- Saturday and Sunday: closed

## After hours
Any time outside the hours above, plus the holidays below. Messages left after hours are followed up the next business day.

## Holidays (front desk closed)
- Thursday, November 26, 2026: Thanksgiving
- Friday, November 27, 2026: day after Thanksgiving
- Friday, December 25, 2026: Christmas Day
- Friday, January 1, 2027: New Year's Day
- Monday, May 31, 2027: Memorial Day
- Monday, July 5, 2027: Independence Day (observed)
- Monday, September 6, 2027: Labor Day

## Access
Members with a key card have 24/7 access, including weekends and holidays.
