# Directions and parking

## Address
500 Sample Street, Suite 210, Springfield, Oregon 97477

## Finding us
- The entrance is on Sample Street, next to the bakery.
- Take the elevator to the 2nd floor; Suite 210 is on the left.
- For turn-by-turn directions, put the address into a map app.

## Parking
- Free visitor spots marked "Sunny Desk" behind the building.
- If they're full: the city-run paid lot across the street.
- Accessible spots are next to the rear entrance.
