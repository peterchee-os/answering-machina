# Operator: Grok Bot

Grok Bot is the original operator, and it pairs with xAI's Grok Voice Agent Builder. Nothing about it changed in the multi-platform layout.

[![Add Answering Machina to Grok Bot](https://img.shields.io/badge/Add_Answering_Machina-to_Grok_Bot-black?style=for-the-badge)](https://x.ai/bot/FUSB3whX23EEO5aiyTk0P)

- **Add the template** with the button above. Grok Bot starts the interview on its own.
- **Or** give Grok Bot the seven playbooks in [`skills/`](../../skills/) and ask it to run **getting-started**.
- Step by step: [Setup with Grok Bot](../../README.md#setup-with-grok-bot-step-by-step) in the main README.
- Platform details: [platforms/xai](../../platforms/xai/README.md).

The playbooks in `skills/` are written for Grok Bot and the xAI console. Their interview, file-writing, review and refresh steps are platform-neutral. Their console steps are specific to xAI. To build on Retell instead, use the [ChatGPT](../chatgpt-dot/INSTRUCTIONS.md) or [Claude](../claude/README.md) operator, or follow the [Retell guide](../../platforms/retell/README.md) by hand.
